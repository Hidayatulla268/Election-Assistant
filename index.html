The Election Process

India
1

Voter Registration (Form 6)

To vote in India, you must be an Indian citizen, 18 years or older on the qualifying date (usually Jan 1st). Apply for inclusion of your name in the electoral roll using Form 6.

2

Verify Electoral Roll

Check if your name is on the electoral roll (voter list) online via the Election Commission of India (ECI) portal or NVSP to ensure you can cast your vote.

3

Find Your Polling Booth

Locate your designated polling booth. You can use your Voter ID (EPIC number) on the ECI website or via SMS to find your exact booth location.

4

Voting on Election Day

Bring your Voter ID or any approved photo ID. Follow the queue, get your finger inked, and press the button on the Electronic Voting Machine (EVM) against your chosen candidate. Check the VVPAT slip to verify your vote.

Announcement & Code of Conduct

The Election Commission announces the schedule. The Model Code of Conduct immediately comes into effect to ensure fair elections.

Filing Nominations

Candidates submit their nomination papers and affidavits detailing their assets, liabilities, and criminal records (if any).

Scrutiny & Withdrawal

Nomination papers are scrutinized. Valid candidates are given a window to withdraw their nominations if they choose.

Campaigning

Candidates campaign up to 48 hours before polling begins. This period is for public meetings, rallies, and media outreach.

Polling Day

Voters cast their votes using EVMs across designated polling booths under tight security.

Counting & Results

Votes are counted securely. The candidate with the highest number of votes in the constituency is declared the winner.
